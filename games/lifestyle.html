I'm Chris Kosco. I'm trying to raise money for a new game called "Lifestyle". Lifestyle is a life-simulation and strategy game where every decision shapes your future. You start with almost nothing, and through hard work, relationships, and choices, you'll build your way up—or fall into struggle. Will you chase money, fame, or happiness? Will you take risks for big rewards, or play it safe and steady?
Create Your Character – Design who you are, from your appearance to your background. Dynamic Life Paths – Choose your career, relationships, hobbies, and side hustles. Realistic Challenges – Manage bills, health, education, and time. Every decision has consequences. Multiple Endings – Success, burnout, wealth, love, loneliness… Your choices shape your outcome. Immersive Mini-Games – From interviews to side jobs, experience interactive challenges that reflect real life. Replayability – Each playthrough creates a unique story.
Most life-sim games focus only on the fantasy of success—big houses, perfect families, dream jobs. Lifestyle shows both the struggle and the reward, making each choice meaningful. It's about the real grind, and the real satisfaction when you achieve your goals.
We want Lifestyle to be the ultimate life-simulation experience, blending the freedom of sandbox games with the depth of RPG storytelling. Whether you want to roleplay as a workaholic CEO, a struggling artist, or just try to balance life's chaos, Lifestyle lets you live it your way.
Why We Need You! Making a game like this takes time, resources, and passion. With your support, we can: Expand the game's world with more careers, events, and endings. Add multiplayer modes where friends can share a life journey together. Create a polished, beautiful experience for PC (and eventually consoles & mobile).
Help bring Lifestyle to life. This is more than a game—it's a chance to explore the highs and lows of real life, and make choices that matter.
